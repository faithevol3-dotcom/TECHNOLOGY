Where technology helps most

Six parts of life that look very different from twenty years ago.

Communication

Messages, voice notes and video calls keep families and teams close, even across countries and time zones.

Health

Phones and watches track steps, sleep and heart rate. Telehealth lets you speak to a doctor without the journey.

Money

Mobile money and banking apps let you pay bills, send cash and save from your phone in seconds.

Learning

Free courses, video lessons and AI tutors make it possible to learn almost any skill at your own pace.

Getting around

Maps show traffic in real time, ride apps find a driver, and digital tickets replace paper.

Home

Smart bulbs, solar systems and phone-controlled appliances help cut waste and save electricity.
